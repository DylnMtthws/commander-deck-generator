# Selection intelligence repair

Quality takes priority over latency. Source investigation reproduced 99/99 benchmark
cards entering the legal priced pool but only 40 surviving coarse role/price pruning.

## Ownership

Coordinator: candidate retention, executable commander packages, scoring/objective,
profile/review grounding, template integration, offline paid evaluation and review.
Claude Opus Medium: conditional card mechanics/payment options; public evidence cache.
Cursor: final-disposition audit and reviewed/unreviewed verdict helpers.
Agents work in isolated source-only folders; the coordinator reviews every diff.

## Acceptance

* No price dominance across distinct functions; bounded diverse candidates with
  requested/verified packages preserved through pruning, swaps and final review.
* Printed mana value stays distinct from conditional payment and noncast activation.
* Commander damage/draw engine, tutor prerequisites, face choices and source
  conditions are explicit. Unknown does not mean supported or impossible.
* Empirical selection evidence carries source/cohort/time/sample provenance. cEDH
  evidence does not silently become general Commander. Inclusion is not win equity.
* Model prose cannot certify a rules interaction, price, card count or winning line.
* No default numeric fit score is presented as an actual model verdict.
* Validate on EDHREC current top ten with varied power/budget plus Vivi regression.
  Inspect outputs, not just tests or list overlap. Record remaining failures honestly.
* Production and existing main/QA URLs remain unchanged pending reviewed release.
