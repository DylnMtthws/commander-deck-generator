## Decision

Mechanical validation passes; broad strategic acceptance fails. Do not promote the coverage policy generally from this cohort. Preserve all outcomes. Next fixes must distinguish actual draw activation cost, prerequisites, commander-supported acceleration, filtering and variable card advantage before counting package coverage. A shared unknown bucket avoids per-card novelty credit but still cannot model 96 heterogeneous draw conditions correctly.
